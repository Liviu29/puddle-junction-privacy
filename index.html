Puddle Junction Studio – Privacy Policy

Effective 29 October 2026 · Last updated 29 October 2026

In short: Puddle Junction Studio is a private tool. The creator of Puddle Junction uses it to publish their own videos to their own Facebook Page and Instagram account, and to read the statistics of those posts. It does not collect data about the people who watch, follow, like or comment on Puddle Junction, and it never sells or shares data.

1. Who is responsible

Puddle Junction is an independent personal creative project, not a company. The data controller is Liviu Nita, an individual based in Romania.

Contact: puddlejunction.studio@gmail.com

2. What Puddle Junction Studio is

Puddle Junction Studio is a Meta app (app ID 2172511723648414). It runs only on the creator's own computer, and nobody else uses it. Its permissions have Meta's Standard Access: only people with a role on the app can grant them, and the creator is the app's only administrator. It is not offered to the public.

It works only with these accounts:

  • the Puddle Junction Facebook Page;
  • the Instagram account linked to that Page.

3. Meta data it accesses, and why

Meta permissionWhat it is used for
pages_show_listFind the Puddle Junction Page among the Pages the creator manages.
pages_manage_postsPublish or schedule the creator's own Reels, videos and photos on the Page, with their caption, cover image, subtitles and AI-content label.
pages_read_engagementRead back the creator's own posts: their IDs, links and whether they are published or scheduled.
read_insightsRead aggregate statistics of the creator's own posts. These include views and plays, the organic/paid split, average watch time, retention and follows gained. Meta reports them only as totals, not per person.
instagram_basicList the creator's own Instagram posts: their IDs, captions, links and like and comment counts.
instagram_content_publishPublish the creator's own Reels to the linked Instagram account.
public_profileGranted automatically by Meta at sign-in. The tool keeps only the creator's own Facebook user ID, so it knows whose authorization it holds.

What it does not access:

  • comment text, or the names or profiles of people who comment, react, follow or watch;
  • messages;
  • followers' identities;
  • other people's Pages or accounts.

It does not use Meta data for advertising, profiling or any purpose other than publishing and measuring the creator's own content.

4. Personal data

The only personal data this tool processes belongs to the creator:

  • their Facebook user ID;
  • the Page access token created from the authorization they granted (the tool keeps only this Page token, not their personal user token);
  • the time they granted it.

The post statistics are aggregate numbers about the creator's own content. They do not identify viewers.

The legal basis is the creator's legitimate interest in publishing and measuring their own work (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

5. Where data is stored and for how long

Everything is stored only on the creator's own computer, in local files and a local database. There is no server or cloud database behind this tool. Copies may exist in the creator's personal backups of that computer.

  • Access token: kept until the creator revokes the app's access, or until it is replaced by a new authorization.
  • Post IDs, publishing records and post statistics: kept while the Puddle Junction project is active, as its publishing history. They are deleted on request (see Data deletion) or when the project ends.

6. Services involved

  • Meta Platforms provides Facebook, Instagram and the Graph API that the tool calls. Meta's own Privacy Policy covers how Meta handles data on its platforms.
  • Anthropic (Claude Code). The creator uses an AI coding assistant on their computer to run the publishing tool. Post IDs, captions and aggregate statistics can pass through it while it works. Access tokens stay in local files and are never written into reports.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this page. GitHub may log visits to it under its own privacy statement. This page sets no cookies and has no analytics.

No data is sold, rented or shared with anyone else.

7. Children

Puddle Junction makes gentle cartoons for families. This tool does not collect any data from viewers of any age, including children.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to:

  • access, correct or delete personal data about you;
  • restrict or object to its processing;
  • receive a copy of it.

Write to puddlejunction.studio@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Romanian data protection authority, ANSPDCP, or to the authority in your own country.

9. Data deletion instructions

Only the creator ever signs in to Puddle Junction Studio. If you have signed in to it, or believe it holds data about you, you can remove it in two ways.

  1. Remove the app. On Facebook, go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations (or Apps and websites), find Puddle Junction Studio, and choose Remove. The app's access token stops working immediately.
  2. Ask for deletion. Email puddlejunction.studio@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request". The stored token and any records linked to your Facebook user ID are deleted within 30 days, and you get an email confirming it.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, this page is updated and the date at the top changes with it.
